Theorem
	: Let $\mathbf{FMan}$ be the category whose objects are simply
      connected smooth manifolds with flat connection, and whose
      arrows are diffeomorphisms that preserve the connection. Let
      $\mathbf{Aff}$ be the category of affine spaces and affine
      maps. Then $\mathbf{FlMan}$ and $\mathbf{Aff}$ are equivalent
      categories.
