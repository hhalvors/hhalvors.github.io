(p 104) In the statement of Existential Elimination, the formula
should be $\exists x\,\phi (x)$ with an "$x$" after the existential
quantifier.

(p 145) The argument here shows the uniqueness of intersection (if it
exists), but there needs to be a separate argument for the existence
of the intersection. Such an argument can be given using the naive
comprehension axiom, or more rigorously, the Axiom of Separation.

(p 146, line 1) The statement should be $A\subseteq B$ if and only
if $A\cap B=A$.

(p 170, line -3) The statement of "totality" should read $\forall
x\forall y(x\neq y\to (Rxy\vee Ryx))$.

(p 215, line 18) "completeness" ⇝ "complete"

(p 232) The statement of EE should include the restriction that the
arbitrary name "$a$" does not occur in the conclusion $\psi$ of the
subproof.
