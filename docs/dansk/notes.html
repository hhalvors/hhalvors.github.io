Danish Texts — Notes and Essays

Scholarly commentary on the texts in the catalog. Notes describe a single work — what our edition covers, the editorial decisions behind it, and what is in the book. Essays argue for a conclusion, usually across several works. Both are written and kept in the danish-texts repository, beside the texts they discuss.

Essays

Before the Heterogeneous Magnitudes

Rasmus Nielsen in May 1849

Nielsen's doctrine that faith and knowledge are uensartede Størrelser is usually credited to his encounter with Kierkegaard. But he states heterogeneity of the natural and the supernatural, in his own emphasis, in a book finished before he reviewed the Postscript — and in the review the word is still largely Kierkegaard's, being quoted. Dating the pivot from the three books of 1849–50.

Kristian Kroman

»Relativitetsprincipet« (1915)

Kroman's rejection of special relativity opened the only sustained critical discussion the theory received in Denmark. On the shape of that debate in Fysisk Tidsskrift, on Høffding's reply to it in »Relation som Kategori«, and on why the article was so hard to find.
