You may use any of the rules of inference, including reductio ad
absurdum, to prove the following sequents.

1. $(P\wedge Q)\to \neg Q\:\vdash\: P\to \neg Q$

2. $\neg (P\to Q)\:\vdash\: P$

3. $\neg (P\to Q)\:\vdash\: Q\to R$

4. $P→Q⊢¬P∨Q$

5. $(P∧¬P)∨Q⊢Q$

6. $P→(Q∨R)⊢¬R→(P→Q)$
