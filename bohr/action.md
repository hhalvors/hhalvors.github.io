Disambiguate: *Action* is a physical quantity that encapsulates the
dynamics of a system. This quantity is the central one in the quantum
revolution, and we will have many things to say about it. But first we
want to talk about action in the even more general sense.

- Wikipedia article on Frederik Sibbern: "Viden er til syvende og
  sidst kun til for at skabe mere fuldkommen *handlen*, mener
  [Sibbern], og med denne tanke for øje har han formet alle sine
  værker."
  
- "What I really lack is to be clear in my mind what I am to do, not
  what I am to know, except in so far as a certain knowledge must
  precede every action." (Kierkegaard)
