---
title: "David Favrholdt"
author: Hans Halvorson and Anja Skaar Jacobsen
---

David Favrholdt was Niels Bohr's primary philosophical exponent from
the 1970s until his death in 2012. We have learned a lot from
Favrholdt, but we nonetheless take some exception to his
historiography --- which focuses on establishing Bohr's
**originality**, and hence **discontinuity** between Bohr and all
philosophers before him. To Favrholdt's credit, he was convinced that
Bohr had some important new ideas. We don't disagree with that; we
just think that "new" does preclude "connected to, or motivated by,
something already existing".
