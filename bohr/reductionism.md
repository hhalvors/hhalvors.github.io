---
title: Reductionism
author: Hans Halvorson and Anja Skaar Jacobsen
---

Bohr's views about complementarity capture part of the sense in which
he is a non-reductionist. But the puzzle to be solved is how Bohr
thinks he can maintain the "unity of science" --- which was a very
important theme for him. In particular, Bohr did not promote the idea
of disconnected layers of reality with their own laws, and he never
used words like "emergence" or "autonomy" when it came to speaking
about the relationship between the macro and micro worlds.
