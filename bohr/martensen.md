---
title: Hans Lassen Martensen
---

He's an incidental figure in the story of Niels Bohr, but he's partly
responsible for the fact that philosophers starting fighting about the
relationship between science and faith, and between objective and
subjective knowledge. M. didn't see the kind of extreme dilemma that
Kierkegaard (and Nielsen) did.

- Martensen, H. **Den christelige Dogmatik**. Reitzel, 1849.
- Martensen, Hans, Curtis L. Thompson, and David J. Kangas. **Between
  Hegel and Kierkegaard: Hans L. Martensen's philosophy of
  religion**. Vol. 17. Oxford University Press, 1997.
