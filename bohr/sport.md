title: "Niels Bohr and sports"

Niels Bohr was unusual --- for a scientist and general intellectual
--- in his high level of engagement with sports and physical
fitness. As a young man, he was an avid soccer player. As an adult, he
was active in ice skating, sailing, and cross country skiing.
