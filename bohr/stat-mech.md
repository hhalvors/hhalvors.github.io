- Bohr was known to hold a special interest for Gibbs' approach to
  thermodynamics.[^1]
  
- Open question: is Bohr ideologically committed to Gibbs' defintion
  of entropy in a way that would put him in opposition to the view of
  [@goldstein2020gibbs]? (That book contains state-of-the-art
  discussion of the relation between statistical mechanics and thermodynamics.)  
  
- Bohr claimed that thermodynamics presages the full-blown
  complementarity between quantities in quantum mechanics; in
  particular, energy and temperature are "almost complementary"
  quantities. See the first-hand account in [@bohr1932faraday] and the
  second-hand account in [@heisenberg2013teil]. The Heisenberg book is
  available in English as *Physics and Beyond*, but the translation is
  not very good. See also [@lindhard1982; @lindhard1986comp]
  
- Uffink and van Lith [-@uffink1999thermo] dispute that claim that
  energy and temperature are complementary.

[^1]: Personal communication with Tomas Bohr.
