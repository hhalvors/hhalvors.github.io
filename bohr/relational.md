---
title: "Carlo Rovelli meets Niels Bohr"
author: Hans Halvorson and Anja Skaar Jacobsen
---

Not many physicists these days are interested in working out a
philosophical worldview that is consistent with their theoretical
practice. One notable exception is Carlo Rovelli, who has developed
"relationalism" into a full blown metaphysics (or
epistemology). Rovelli's approach has many similarities to Bohr's,
although some notable differences remain.
