Einstein and Bohr had been debating the foundations of quantum
mechanics since 1927. On several occasions, Einstein had suggested
thought experiments to show that there was some or other problem with
the theory. The Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen argument of 1935 was
Einstein's final attempt.
