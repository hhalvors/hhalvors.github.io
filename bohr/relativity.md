---
title: "The theory of relativity"
author: Hans Halvorson and Anja Skaar Jacobsen
---

- Niels Bohr sees both continuity and discontinuity between relativity
  theory and quantum physics.[^1] He often uses relativity theory as a
  foil to illustrate the "epistemological lesson" of quantum physics
  --- and his discussions show that he has an interesting take on how
  to understand relativity theory itself.

- "You are certainly right that Einstein is not consequent
  [consistent] when speaking of the ideal of detached observer and
  neglecting his own wisdom of relativity, which Eddington poetically
  described by the picture of how long man traced a footprint in the
  sand until he recognized that it was his own." (Bohr to Pauli, March
  25, 1955)
